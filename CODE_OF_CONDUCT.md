# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Rakit contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members are expected to make project
spaces welcoming, professional, and useful to people with different backgrounds and experience
levels.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith while evidence is gathered.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, identities, and differences in experience.
- Accept correction and update claims when tests or documentation show they were wrong.
- Keep security disclosures private until coordinated release.
- Focus disagreement on the work rather than the person doing it.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized comments, stalking, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, sustained personal attacks, or publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable. Repeatedly disrupting project spaces after a maintainer request to
stop is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project chat, and
other spaces where someone is representing the Rakit project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit/remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed
to protect project spaces. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately when
appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters unnecessarily.

For conduct reports, contact the maintainers through an available private GitHub-supported channel.
Do not publish personal or sensitive incident details in a public issue merely to request contact.
Security vulnerabilities follow the separate process in `SECURITY.md`.
